Platinenlupe Flasher

Flash both boards from the browser — Chrome or Edge on a computer, one USB cable. Nothing to install.

1 · ESP32 — loupe side

Talks to the loupe over Bluetooth and its WiFi.

2 · Home side — NodeMCU (ESP8266) or second ESP32

Serves the page — as its own hotspot or in your home network.

3 · Wire and go

  1. Unplug both. Wire ESP32 GPIO17 → NodeMCU D7, GPIO16 ← D8, GND — G, VIN ← VU.
  2. Power the NodeMCU over USB. Switch the loupe on.
  3. Join the WiFi Platinenlupe-XXXX, password platinenlupe. The loupe opens by itself.
  4. Change the hotspot password under Device settings.

Flash one board at a time and pull the 5 V wire while flashing. Web flashing needs HTTPS (or localhost).
